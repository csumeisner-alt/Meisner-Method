# Sound Asset Sources

## eagle_screech.wav
- **Source:** "Golden Eagle (Aquila chrysaetos) (W1CDR0001387 BD6)"  
  Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden_Eagle_(Aquila_chrysaetos)_(W1CDR0001387_BD6).ogg
- **Recorded by:** British Library Wildlife Sound Archive (date: 1969)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Processing:** Extracted 2 s call segment (0.65–2.65 s), converted to stereo 22050 Hz WAV, applied loudness normalization and short echo tail for cinematic depth.

## gunfire_burst.wav
- **Source:** "9 mm gunshot - Mike Koenig"  
  Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:9_mm_gunshot-mike-koenig-123.wav
- **Recorded by:** Mike Koenig (soundbible.com)
- **License:** CC BY 3.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- **Processing:** Single-shot recording trimmed to 0.35 s transient, replicated 5 times at 155 ms intervals, assembled into a 0.9 s burst, normalized to stereo 22050 Hz WAV.
