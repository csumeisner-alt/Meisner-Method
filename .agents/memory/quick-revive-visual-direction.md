name: Quick Revive visual direction
description: The approved visual and audio direction for the Quick Revive collectible and redemption moment.
---

Quick Revive is a glowing cyan-blue glass soda bottle: dark cap, blue neck band, carbonated bubbles, and a large circular “REVIVE / SODA” label with a simple white emblem. Its redemption is a fizzy lift, bubble release, and blue splash—not a generic potion effect.

**Why:** The user supplied a reference image to replace the prior lightning-bottle interpretation and wants the collectible to feel like a luminous branded soda.

**How to apply:** Keep Quick Revive’s visual language blue, glassy, carbonated, and label-led. Its supplied redemption audio starts with the animation in supported native builds while sound is enabled; preserve the Expo Go native-audio guard.
