description: Approved Dave Ramsey Daiquiri bottle and redemption animation direction.

The Dave Ramsey Daiquiri should read as a cinematic collectible bottle: navy glass, bright carbonated gold liquid, blue-and-gold label, gold R neck medallion, oval Daiquiri label, visible bubbles, and a warm glow.

**Why:** The supplied reference replaced the earlier flat light-blue bottle treatment and gives this premium reward a distinct identity inside the Central Bank.

**How to apply:** Keep the bottle recognizable at compact mobile sizes and pair redemption with a short, high-impact sequence: rise and rock, neck glint, liquid surge, bubbles, label flare, then a controlled golden splash/ripple. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.
