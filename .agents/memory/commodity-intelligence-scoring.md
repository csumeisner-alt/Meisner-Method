---
name: Commodity intelligence scoring
description: Stable interpretation rules for weighted commodity intelligence scores and missing evidence.
---

The commodity intelligence score is a present-evidence assessment, not a future-return forecast. Each commodity family uses its configured category weights; unavailable category weight stays neutral and is never redistributed. Volatility is a stability measure that can subtract from the overall score but cannot increase it.

**Why:** Missing commodity fundamentals, physical balances, and broader market context are common; treating missing inputs as zero or silently reallocating their weight would make sparse evidence look more decisive.

**How to apply:** When adding official commodity sources or changing category calculations, preserve missing-data coverage disclosures and the non-positive effect of risk.
