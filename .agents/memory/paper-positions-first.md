**Rule:** In Meisner Method Paper Trading, open Portfolio to current Positions first, keep position rows and all three simulated-account summaries compact, and keep the Event Arena summary short enough to leave at least four position rows visible when the screen allows. **Why:** The user wants holdings immediately visible with less scrolling, and specifically asked to shorten both the Event Arena and simulated-account summaries to save space.

**How to apply:** Keep Positions as the initial view while placing its tab after Overview; preserve separate accessible tabs, keep the count account-scoped, optimize collapsed rows and account summaries for phone-height density, and avoid redundant Event Arena status or rank copy.
