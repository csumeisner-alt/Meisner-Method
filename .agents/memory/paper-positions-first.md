**Rule:** In Meisner Method Paper Trading, open Portfolio to current Positions first and keep position rows compact enough to target four visible on a typical phone screen.

**Why:** The user wants holdings immediately visible and less scrolling; the position count already appears in the Positions section.

**How to apply:** Keep Positions first in the Portfolio secondary navigation, make it the initial view, and preserve position details and actions while optimizing collapsed rows for phone-height density.
