name: Paper Trading positions-first layout
description: The user's preferred first view and density for the Meisner Method Paper Trading screen.
---

**Rule:** In Meisner Method Paper Trading, open Portfolio to current Positions first, keep position rows compact, and show the active account's count in the selected Positions tab rather than repeating a heading above the cards.

**Why:** The user wants holdings immediately visible and less scrolling, with the Positions count beside Overview in the Portfolio navigation.

**How to apply:** Keep Positions as the initial view while placing its tab after Overview; preserve separate accessible tabs, keep the count account-scoped, and optimize collapsed rows for phone-height density.
