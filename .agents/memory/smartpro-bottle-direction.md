---
name: SmartPro bottle direction
description: Approved SmartPro Soda bottle identity shared by canvas mockups and the production mobile app.
---

SmartPro Soda uses vivid lime-green liquid inside dark green glass, a black cap, and a rectangular black label with a large white “SP”, a thin green divider, and small “SMARTPRO” lettering.

**Why:** The user rejected the older circular “S / SMARTPRO / SODA” badge as a legacy version after investing in the newer canvas design.

**How to apply:** Treat the approved canvas bottle as the source of truth for every SmartPro thumbnail, inventory row, inspection view, and redemption animation in the APK. Locked states may desaturate it, but unlocked states must retain the vivid green color.
