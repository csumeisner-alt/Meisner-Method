---
name: Meisner Method branding
description: Public naming and compatibility guidance for the app's rebrand.
---

The user-facing product name is **Meisner Method**. Do not introduce or restore the former StockSense branding in visible copy, artifact titles, launcher metadata, or service identification strings.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants one consistent public brand across the app and its surrounding project surfaces.

**How to apply:** Keep legacy local-storage namespaces unchanged unless a migration is deliberately added; renaming those keys without migration would discard existing watchlists, alerts, trades, and unlock state.
